# 🧘 Zen Eigo

**Zen Eigo** is a minimalist, tap-to-build English listening comprehension app designed specifically for Japanese speakers.

It flips the original Zen-Listen concept (learning Japanese) into an English-learning tool. Users listen to a sentence, tap the correct word chunks to build the sentence, and learn from grammar hints—all without the pressure of a timer.

## ✨ Features

- **CEFR Levels (A1–C1):** Content is graded from beginner (A1) to advanced (C1), with over 150 carefully crafted sentences.
- **Distractor Words:** The word bank includes grammatically tricky or phonetically similar "distractor" words to ensure you are truly listening and not just guessing.
- **Local Progress Tracking:** The app uses `localStorage` to save your progress. If you get a sentence 100% correct on the _first try_, it is marked as "Mastered" and won't appear again.
- **Level Complete Modals:** Clear a whole level, and you'll be prompted to move to the next CEFR level or reset the current one.
- **Audio Controls:** Toggle between normal and slow audio speeds. Word-by-word audio playback is also available.
- **Japanese UI & Hints:** Full Katakana readings, Japanese translations, and grammar notes tailored for Japanese learners.
